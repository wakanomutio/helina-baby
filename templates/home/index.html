The truth is your first carrier usually isn't your last. Most carriers are built for one stage, which somehow means buying another one six months later. And then another.

We Hear You.

Helina Baby skips that whole expensive, mind bending cycle. It adjusts to fit your 8 pound newborn to a 40 pound toddler and fits bodies of all shapes and sizes. So when it’s someone else’s turn to carry, just pass the baby. No resizing. No readjusting. No figuring out whose settings were whose.

WHY HELINA?

Informed by Science

Designed to carry comfortably, for longer

Helina Baby is built around anatomy, biomechanics and physics. Not because those words sound impressive, but because they matter when you’re carrying a growing human.

Every part of the carrier has a purpose. The Helina HUGFIT™ System adjusts to your baby’s body to support them in the M position as they grow. The X-shaped straps distribute your baby’s weight across your body, not one spot. And we chose undyed organic cotton because babies put everything in their mouths. Including their carrier.
